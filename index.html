Sol Shift

You are in charge of a small base on Mars. Keep it running for 30 sols until the next ship arrives.

A sol is one day on Mars. It is about 24 hours and 40 minutes long, a little longer than a day on Earth.

Keep three things from running out:

  • Life support: air, water and food.
  • Power: electricity from solar panels.
  • Shielding: protection from radiation, invisible energy from the Sun and space that can harm the body.

And watch your crew. Tired people make more mistakes, and mistakes cost life support.
