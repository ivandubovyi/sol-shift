      Where the facts come from

      Real facts in this game, each checked on the page linked:

        Photos of Mars

        The mountain and the sunset behind the base are real photos taken on Mars by NASA's Curiosity rover. The base, the crew and the ground in front are drawn. NASA and JPL let anyone use their photos with the credit shown. NASA and JPL do not endorse this game.

          Game numbers

          How much each choice costs, how fast meters drop, and how tiredness turns into mistakes are game-design choices made to keep the game fair and fun. They are not NASA facts. They are listed in data/numbers.js with the label "game-design". The sky colors by day and at night are artistic choices too. The daytime photo had its sky removed, because NASA adjusted that photo so its sky looks blue like Earth's.

          Made with AI help

          The code for this game was written by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) from the team's plan. There are no AI-generated pictures in the game. The labels and numbers use the Orbitron font (SIL Open Font License). See AI-USE.md.
